## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What is Vocabia?
**A:** Vocabia is your smart vocabulary assistant that helps you look up, save, and master English words.
You can explore detailed definitions or let our AI create personalized exercises to test your knowledge.

---

### Q: How does the AI Exercise feature work?
**A:** When you have at least 10 words in your "Saved Words" list, Vocabia's AI generates a fill-in-the-blank quiz using those words. You get 3 lives to guess the correct word for each sentence.

---

### Q: Can I save my favorite words?
**A:** Yes! You can tap the heart icon on any word's detail page to save it to your "Saved" tab. This data is stored locally on your device using Core Data.

---

### Q: Do I need an internet connection to use Vocabia?
**A:** You can view your saved words offline at any time. However, searching for new words, listening to audio, and using the AI Exercise feature require an internet connection.
